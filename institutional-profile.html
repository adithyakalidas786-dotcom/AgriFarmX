Institutional Profile

AgriFarmX is a pioneering institution dedicated to the advancement of sustainable agriculture through expert consultancy and research. Established with the vision of bridging the gap between scientific innovation and traditional farming practices, we have grown into a trusted partner for farmers across the region.

Our Legacy

Since our inception, AgriFarmX has focused on providing evidence-based solutions to complex agricultural challenges. Our team of experienced consultants, ranging from soil microbiologists to crop management experts, works tirelessly to ensure that every farmer has access to the best advice.

Key Highlights

  • State-of-the-art soil testing laboratories.
  • Network of over 50 expert agricultural consultants.
  • Over 10,000 farmers assisted annually.
  • Pioneers in organic pest control and yield maximization strategies.
